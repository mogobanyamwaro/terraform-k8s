# 01. Telemetry Data

**Domain:** Fundamentals of Observability (18%)

## Concept Refresher

**Observability** is the ability to infer internal state from **telemetry** (data the system emits). **Monitoring** watches known symptoms (dashboards/alerts you designed in advance).

**Three signals** in OpenTelemetry:

| Signal | Unit | Answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Traces** | Span (in a trace) | Where time went across services |
| **Metrics** | Measurement / time series | How much, how often, SLO burn |
| **Logs** | Log record | What exactly happened |

**Baggage** is propagated key/value **context**, not a fourth signal. **Profiles** exist in the wider ecosystem; OTCA’s core three are traces, metrics, logs.

**Correlation:** a log or metric exemplar can carry `trace_id` / `span_id` so you jump from “error rate up” to “this request.”

**Resource:** *who* emitted the data (`service.name`, `service.version`, `k8s.pod.name`). Distinct from span/metric **attributes** (the operation).

Push vs pull: OTel apps typically **push OTLP** to a Collector; Prometheus **pulls**. The Collector can scrape Prometheus *and* receive OTLP.
