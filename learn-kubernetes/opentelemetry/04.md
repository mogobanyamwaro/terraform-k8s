# 04. Analysis and Outcomes

**Domain:** Fundamentals of Observability (18%)

## Concept Refresher

Telemetry is useless unless it drives **outcomes**: find latency, errors, saturation; prove SLOs; debug a *single* request.

**RED** (services): Rate, Errors, Duration.  
**USE** (resources): Utilization, Saturation, Errors.

Traces explain a slow **request**. Metrics explain **fleet** health and alerts. Logs explain **payload/exception** detail.

Exemplars / trace_id on metrics connect RED alerts to a trace.

SLI/SLO: often from RED (availability = 1 − errors/rate; latency from histogram or span duration). OTel does not replace an SLO product; it **feeds** it.

Cardinality: too many metric attributes → expensive, slow PromQL/Mimir. Keep identity on **resource**, bounded enums on metrics, high-card on traces/logs.

Sampling **changes** what you can analyse (you may miss rare errors unless you tail-sample errors or use error-aware sampling).
