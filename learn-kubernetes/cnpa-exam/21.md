# 21. AI/ML in Platform Automation

**Domain:** IDPs and Developer Experience (8%)

## Concept Refresher

CNPA includes **AI/ML in platform automation** as an associate-level **use-case** competency, not an MLOps PhD.

**Sensible uses**

| Use | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Assistive DX | Chat over **catalog/docs/runbooks** (RAG on *your* platform) |
| Toil reduction | Suggest golden-path template from a natural-language request, still **creates a PR** |
| Ops assist | Summarise incidents; propose rollback **with human confirm** |
| Policy/explain | “Why was my deploy blocked?” from admission messages |
| Capacity | Forecast runner/node demand (careful) |

**Guardrails (exam)**

- AI must not be an unauthenticated `cluster-admin`
- Outputs go through **Git/PR and policy** like any other change
- Training data: no leaking secrets from logs
- Hallucinated kubectl is **worse** than a ticket if applied blindly
- Platform still owns **deterministic** golden paths; AI is an **interface**, not the source of truth

MLOps platforms (training jobs, GPUs, Kubeflow) are **another** golden path for data teams — same product thinking, extra hardware APIs.

## Exam tips

- Ground in **catalog APIs**, not the open web.
- ML hardware = just another **path**.
