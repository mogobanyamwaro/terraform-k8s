# 21. Policy Reports

**Domain:** Policy Management (10%)

## Concept Refresher

**PolicyReport** (namespaced) and **ClusterPolicyReport** hold results in the **policy report** / OpenReports model.

Each result: **policy**, **rule**, **resource**, **result** (`pass` | `fail` | `warn` | `error` | `skip`), message, timestamp, scored.

Sources: admission and **background** scans. `reports-controller` aggregates.

```bash
kubectl get policyreport -A
kubectl get clusterpolicyreport
```

Audit mode **depends** on reports to see violations. Enforce still writes reports plus denies.

Don’t use reports as the only HA signal. Prometheus metrics complement them (`22.md`).

Reports can be large — the reports-controller needs resources; some installs disable reports.
