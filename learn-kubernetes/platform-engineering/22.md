# 22. Measuring the Platform (DORA and Productivity)

**Domain:** Measuring your Platform (8%)

## Concept Refresher

If you cannot measure, you cannot treat the platform as a product.

### DORA four keys (memorise names and direction)

| Metric | Elite-ish direction | Platform lever |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deployment frequency** | Higher | Golden path, self-service CD, small batches |
| **Lead time for changes** | Lower (commit → prod) | CI speed, preview envs, less waiting on tickets |
| **Change failure rate** | Lower | Tests, progressive delivery, policy, build-once |
| **Time to restore (MTTR)** | Lower | Observability, rollback, IR, status page |

Platforms **move DORA by changing the path**, not by yelling at developers.

### Platform efficiency and team productivity

- Time to first hello-world / first deploy
- % of deploys on the golden path (adoption)
- Platform tickets per team per week (**should fall**)
- Self-service success rate / API SLO
- Onboarding days
- Cognitive load / DX surveys (SPACE: Satisfaction, Performance, Activity, Communication, Efficiency — know SPACE exists)

**Vanity:** plugin count, YAML lines, “we installed Backstage.”

**Misuse:** punishing teams with DORA without fixing the platform; gaming frequency with empty deploys.

FLOW / value-stream: wait time in platform queues is **your** waste.
