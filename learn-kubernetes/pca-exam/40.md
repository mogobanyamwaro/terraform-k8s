## Scenario Answers

**S1: C.** Every other layer checks out, and a mute time interval is the only suppression that leaves the alert looking `active` with no suppression markers while producing zero notifications. `group_wait` would have elapsed and would also show a pending notification.

**S2: B.** A silently empty expression is the classic case. Decompose it and check the actual label names with `count by (label) (metric)`.

**S3: B.** The slow-burn blind spot of static thresholds, and the reason burn-rate alerting exists. At 0.5% sustained, the burn rate is 5x and the budget is gone in six days.

**S4: B.** Globs are re-expanded on reload, so a matching new file **should** load. The fact that other rules still work is the clue: a failed reload leaves the previous config running.

**S5: B.** No batching. Group by `alertname` and `cluster` instead of by `instance`.

**S6: B.** Both replicas emit their own `replica` label, making the alerts non-identical, so Alertmanager cannot deduplicate them. Dropping the label makes them byte-identical.

**S7: B.** Recording rules over the bucket rates. Keeping `le` means the panels can still choose any quantile and re-aggregate.

**S8: B.** First match wins among siblings.

**S9: B.** Evaluation overrun. Also check `prometheus_rule_group_last_duration_seconds > prometheus_rule_group_interval_seconds`.

**S10: B.** No series means no result. `absent()` exists precisely for this.

## Exam tips

The traps this drill is built around:

- **`pending` on the first matching evaluation**, and the **`for` timer resets** on any gap.
- **30s / 5m / 4h** for `group_wait` / `group_interval` / `repeat_interval`, and knowing which one causes which delay.
- A new alert joining a group waits for **`group_interval`**, not `group_wait`.
- **`group_by: ['...']` disables grouping; `group_by: []` merges everything.**
- **First matching sibling route wins**, then matching descends into children. `continue: true` for fan-out.
- Children inherit **receiver, grouping, and timers**, never **matchers**.
- A **missing label matches `!=` and `!~`** in Alertmanager.
- **Silence = human/runtime. Inhibition = another firing alert. Mute interval = clock.** And the mute interval is the one that leaves **no trace** in the API.
- **Omitting `equal`** makes an inhibition rule global.
- Suppression never stops Prometheus from firing. `ALERTS` has **no `suppressed` state**.
- **Alertmanager never sees `$value`.** Put it in an annotation.
- `.GroupLabels` versus `.CommonLabels`.
- **`$__rate_interval`** inside `rate()` in Grafana. Blank short-range graphs mean too few samples.
- **Heatmap + `_bucket` rates** for distributions.
- **Error budget = `1 - SLO`. Threshold = burn rate x budget.** 14.4x on 99.9% is `0.0144`; 6x is `0.006`.
- The **short window** in a burn-rate alert is an `and` guard for fast resolution.
- **Record bucket rates keeping `le`**, never the quantile itself.
- **`absent()`** for vanished targets; `up == 0` cannot see them.
- **Watchdog** for a dead pipeline.
- Duplicates with **identical** content = not clustered. Duplicates with **different** content = divergent configs. Duplicate alerts with **different labels** = `replica` not dropped.
